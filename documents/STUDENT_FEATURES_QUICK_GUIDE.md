# Student Dashboard - Quick Feature Guide

## 🎯 New Features Overview

### 1. Check Sessions
**What it does:** Shows all available sessions for your academic year

**How to access:**
1. Login to student dashboard
2. Click "Check Sessions" in the sidebar
3. Browse sessions designed for your year

**What you'll see:**
- Session title
- Year badge
- Session description
- "Already Registered" badge (if applicable)
- "Take Quiz" button

**What you can do:**
- View all sessions for your year
- Take quizzes for registered sessions
- See which sessions you're already registered for

---

### 2. Suggest a Session
**What it does:** Allows you to suggest new sessions to the IAP team

**How to access:**
1. Login to student dashboard
2. Click "Suggest a Session" in the sidebar
3. Fill out the form with your suggestion

**Form Fields:**
- **Name** - Your full name (pre-filled)
- **Roll Number** - Your roll number (pre-filled)
- **Year** - Your academic year (pre-filled)
- **Branch/Department** - Your department (pre-filled)
- **Section** - Your class section (you fill this)
- **Session You Want** - Name of the session you want (you fill this)
- **Any Other Query** - Additional comments (optional)

**What happens after submission:**
1. Your suggestion is saved
2. You see a success message
3. Admin team reviews it
4. You can check status in admin dashboard

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why are only certain sessions showing?**
A: Sessions are filtered to show only those for your academic year. This ensures you see relevant content.

**Q: Can I suggest any session?**
A: Yes! You can suggest any session you think would be helpful. The admin team will review it.

**Q: What happens to my suggestion?**
A: Your suggestion is saved and reviewed by the admin team. They may approve it to be added as a session.

**Q: Can I change my suggestion after submitting?**
A: Currently, you cannot edit submitted suggestions. You can submit a new one if needed.

**Q: How long does it take for my suggestion to be reviewed?**
A: The admin team typically reviews suggestions within 1-2 weeks.

**Q: Will I be notified when my suggestion is approved?**
A: Currently, you can check the admin dashboard to see the status of your suggestions.
